HOW THIS GAME OF WEREWOLF WORKS (read carefully; do not assume rules from other versions)

$count players each secretly hold a role on one of three sides:
- VILLAGE: wins when every Werewolf-team player and every hostile Neutral is dead.
- WEREWOLVES: win when they equal or outnumber everyone else alive and no hostile Neutral remains.
- NEUTRALS: each has a personal win condition (see their role). Some can win alongside another side.

The game alternates NIGHT and DAY, starting with Night 1.

NIGHT
1. Private chats: some groups (e.g. the Werewolves in their den, Masons, Lovers) talk secretly. Only members see it.
2. Actions: every player with a night ability secretly chooses what to do, or skips. Everyone acts at the same time.
3. Movement: an ability either VISITS a home (interacts with whoever is inside), TRAVELS to a home (watches from outside without interacting), or doesn't move. Some roles react to who comes to a home (a Veteran on alert kills all visitors; a Lookout sees everyone who arrives).
4. Resolution order: Role-Block first, then Protection, Manipulation, Information, Role-Copy, Support, and Killing last. A role-blocked player's action simply fails (a few roles, like the Seer, cannot be blocked).
5. Protection and Role-Block abilities cannot target the same player two nights in a row. Information abilities can.
6. The Werewolf pack's nightly kill is carried out by the ALPHA. If the Alpha dies, another Werewolf-team player becomes Alpha.
7. Information can be manipulated: e.g. a framed player appears as a Wolf to investigators.

DAWN: each player privately receives the results of their night (what they learned, whether they were blocked, attacked, healed...). Deaths are announced publicly; the dead player's role is revealed.

DAY
1. Discussion: everyone talks. Anyone may lie, bluff, or claim any role. Sharing what you learned at night is usually how the village finds the wolves, but it can make you a target.
2. Vote: all living players vote at the same time for one player to eliminate (or "nobody"). The most votes is eliminated; a tie means nobody is. The eliminated player gives last words, then their role is revealed.
3. A few roles also act during the day (e.g. the Mayor).

Dead players are out of the game: they cannot talk, vote or act.
