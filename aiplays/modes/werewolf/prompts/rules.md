WEREWOLF RULES
- $count players sit at a round table. Hidden roles in play: $setup.
- Teams: the Village (Villagers, Seer, Doctor) versus the Werewolves.
- NIGHT: the werewolves secretly agree on one non-werewolf to kill. The Seer secretly learns whether one player is a werewolf. The Doctor secretly protects one player from that night's attack (any player, including themselves, but not the same player two nights in a row).
- DAY: the night's victim is announced. Everyone discusses, then all living players vote at the same time to eliminate one player. The most votes is eliminated (a tie means nobody). Eliminated players reveal their role and leave the game.
- The Village wins when every werewolf is dead. The Werewolves win when they equal or outnumber everyone else.
- Anyone may lie, bluff, or claim any role. Dead players cannot speak.
